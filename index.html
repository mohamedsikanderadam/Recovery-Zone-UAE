Zone 02

The Contrast Zone

Heat, cold, and the swing between the two. Used after training, between sessions, or as a protocol in its own right.

  • SaunaUp to 5 people. Dry heat to raise core temperature and open circulation.
  • Ice BathUp to 2 people. Controlled cold immersion with a therapist setting the exposure.
  • Contrast BathAlternating heat and cold in timed rounds. The cycle is prescribed, not left to you.
  • Massage ChairPercussion and compression, as a warm-down between rounds or on a light day.

The full menu

Seven hands-on treatments, delivered by qualified therapists and chosen for what your body is actually doing — training, rehabilitating, or sitting still for too long. Working pressure is listed so you know what you are walking into.

Deep Tissue MassageDeep

Slow, sustained pressure worked into the deeper muscle layers. For chronic tightness that surface work never reaches — long-standing back, neck and calf load that has stopped responding to anything lighter.

Sports MassageFirm

Built around training load: preparation before competition, recovery after it, and management of overuse strain in between. Paced to where you are in your week, not to a fixed routine.

Lymphatic Drainage MassageLight

Light, rhythmic strokes worked in the direction of lymph flow. Used for post-training and post-operative swelling and fluid retention, where pressure would only make things worse.

Assisted StretchingLight

Therapist-guided stretching that takes joints through range you cannot reach on your own. Stiff hips, locked shoulders, short hamstrings — held long enough to change something.

Myofascial ReleaseModerate

Sustained tension held on restricted fascia until it gives. The aim is glide between tissue layers rather than forcing the muscle underneath.

Myofascial DecompressionNegative

Cupping-based negative pressure. Tissue is lifted away from the layers beneath instead of being compressed into them — useful over stubborn adhesions and scar tissue.

Kinetic Release TherapyFirm

Pressure applied while the limb is moved, so tissue releases through the pattern it actually has to work in — not while lying still.

Light → Deep is pressure applied into the tissue. Negative means tissue is lifted, not compressed. Every session begins with an assessment.
